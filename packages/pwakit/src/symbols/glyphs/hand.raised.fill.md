# hand.raised.fill

File: `hand.raised.fill.svg`.

Authorship: produced 2026-10-10 by a Claude agent (Opus 5.5) for Satin, clean-room. Derived from Framework7 Icons 5.0.5 (MIT) outline geometry: 56-unit viewBox, ~3.8-unit regular stroke, round caps and joins, one nonzero filled path via skia-pathops.

Description: Fill that shares its outline's silhouette: the Framework7 outline's outer silhouette made solid, with its interior detail strokes knocked out. Slash and badge variants are the base fill with a gap cut around a slash or badge fitted to the Framework7 outline; open-box variants are a solid box with the tool cut through. Replaces Framework7's separately drawn fill, which had a different shape.

Construction update (2026-10-10): the outline's palm and thumb silhouette plus four equal round-topped fingers, drawn at the outline's own fingertip heights across its outer finger edges. They are parted by 1.6-unit gaps that end where the outline's finger spurs end. The thumb is parted up its wedge and out through the notch. The fingertips are drawn rather than kept from the outline, because Framework7's tip arcs are centred on the valleys and any cut down the spurs clipped them flat.

Provenance: no SF Symbols file, font, image, screenshot or exported path was opened, traced or recalled. Only Framework7 Icons SVGs were used, as geometry sources or style references.
