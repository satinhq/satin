# paperplane.fill

File: `paperplane.fill.svg`.

Authorship: produced 2026-10-15 by a Claude agent (Opus 5.5) for Satin, clean-room. Derived from Framework7 Icons 5.0.5 (MIT) outline geometry with the set's fill construction (the measured reference, "A `.fill` is its outline filled" and "Fill details are seams, openings and parted pieces"). Base: Framework7 `paperplane`.

Description: The outline's silhouette made solid with one 2.6-unit seam on the fold, opening through the back notch where the outline's fold meets it and stopping a wall short of the tip. The reference face-pair seam collapses to a pinhole here because the fold narrows to nothing at the tip. Replaces Framework7's separately drawn fill (silhouette IoU 0.91).

Method: geometry authored as primitive strokes and shapes, expanded and unioned with skia-pathops into one nonzero filled path, coordinates at 4 decimals. Not scaled for apparent size; set-wide sizing is calibrated in the Symbol system.

Provenance: no SF Symbols file, font, image, screenshot or exported path was opened, traced or recalled for this glyph. Only Framework7 Icons 5.0.5 SVGs (MIT) were viewed, as style references; the Framework7 outline named above is also the geometry source.
